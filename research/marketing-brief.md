# Marketing brief: what hotel buyers need to see

## 1. Home page (2-3 screen heights)

**First 10 seconds:**
- One strong **hotel** image (pool, oceanfront dining, resort from above). Not a landscape.
- One plain line: hotel photos, video and drone.
- One button: "Check availability".

**Contents, in order:**
1. **Hero** (1 screen): image or muted video loop, headline, subhead, button.
2. **6 small photo tiles** (half a screen) linking to Photos, Video, Drone. Hover shows a label.
3. **How it works + why me** (half a screen): 3 steps, 2 lines about working in hotel marketing.
4. **Contact bar**: one line, button, email.

Cut long intros, service essays and full galleries from the home page.

## 2. Pages

- **Photos** (prove range): small grid, filters (Pools, Dining, Rooms, Lifestyle, Property), click to enlarge.
  *Intro:* "Pools, dining, rooms and the people who use them. Shot for your website, booking sites and Instagram."
- **Video** (social-ready): vertical clips that play on hover or tap, plus one wide clip.
  *Intro:* "Short clips made for Reels, Stories and your website. Vertical and wide, ready to post."
- **Drone** (location and scale): hotel and coastline aerials first.
  *Intro:* "Your property from above. Aerial photos and video that show the location, the pool and the view."
- **About** (trust): photo of Justin, short story, deliverables, how booking works.
  *Intro:* "I work in hotel marketing. I started shooting because hotels kept running out of good photos to post."
- **Contact** (easy ask): short form, email, reply time.
  *Intro:* "Tell me about your property and what you need. I'll reply within [JUSTIN TO CONFIRM: 24 hours?]."

## 3. Home headline options

1. **Hotel photos and video your marketing team will actually use.**
   Interiors, lifestyle and drone, shot by someone who works in hotel marketing.
2. **Photos that fill your hotel's feed, website and booking pages.**
   Photo, video and drone shoots that are easy to book and fairly priced.
3. **Hotel photography, made simple.**
   One shoot, one photographer, images ready for every channel.

Pick: option 1. It says who it's for and why you're different.

## 4. Trust builders (no fake claims)

- **"I work in hotel marketing."** Strongest point. [JUSTIN TO CONFIRM: OK to say; name the hotel or not]
- **Deliverables:** "X edited photos, sized for web, Instagram and booking sites." [JUSTIN TO CONFIRM]
- **Turnaround:** "Edited photos in X days." [JUSTIN TO CONFIRM]
- **Usage rights** (buyers check this first): e.g. "Use the photos anywhere you market the hotel, no time limit." [JUSTIN TO CONFIRM]
- **How booking works:** 1) Email or short call. 2) I send a shot list and prep list. 3) Shoot day. 4) Photos delivered.
- **Drone:** FAA Part 107 licensed, insured? [JUSTIN TO CONFIRM]
- **Price signal:** "Starting at $X" or "packages for smaller hotels". [JUSTIN TO CONFIRM]
- **Later:** client quotes and logos, only once real.

## 5. Which photos to lead with

**Lead with:**
- Oceanfront pool aerial at blue hour (best hero).
- Oceanfront restaurant interior, still and vertical video.
- Coastline resort aerials, palms over beach.
- Incoming: pools, dining, lawn events, people using the space.

**Lower down only:**
- Rooftops at blue hour: nice mood, not clearly a hotel.
- iPhone palms and island beach: filler, never hero.

**Never open with:**
- Mountains and alpine lakes. They say "landscape photographer". Put them on Drone, below hotel aerials.
- AI-generated or heavily edited images.
- Scenes with no building, pool or table.

Rule: every image above the fold should make a director think "that could be our property."

## 6. Calls to action and form

**Buttons (same wording site-wide):**
- Main: **"Check availability"**
- Secondary: **"See the work"**
- Footer: justinclosemedia@gmail.com

**Form fields:**
1. Name
2. Email
3. Hotel or property name
4. City
5. What do you need? (Photos, Video, Drone, Not sure)
6. Timing (This month, 1-3 months, Just looking)
7. Message (optional)

Skip budget and phone; ask in the reply. After submit: "Thanks. I'll reply within [JUSTIN TO CONFIRM] with next steps."
