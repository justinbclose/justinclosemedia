# Luxury color palettes

Four directions to choose from. Each one has a background, a text color, and accent colors.

**How to judge them:** picture your best photo on each background. The palette should make the
photo look better and never compete with it.

**Readability check:** "Contrast" measures how easy text is to read. 4.5 or higher is the
standard for normal text. Lower-scoring accents are fine for large headings, lines, and
buttons, but not for paragraphs.

## 1. Sandstone (warm neutrals)

Feels like a boutique desert or Mediterranean resort. It flatters warm interiors and golden-hour light.

| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Background | Ivory | `#F5F0E8` |
| Section background | Sand | `#E4D8C4` |
| Muted accent | Stone | `#A89B87` |
| Accent | Bronze | `#8C6A43` |
| Text | Espresso | `#2B2420` |

Contrast: espresso on ivory is **13.5** (excellent). Bronze on ivory is **4.3** (large text only).

## 2. Nightcap (dark and moody)

Feels like an upscale city hotel bar at night. Photos glow against the dark background. This is
the most dramatic option and the best at standing out.

| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Background | Near-black | `#121212` |
| Section background | Charcoal | `#1E1D1B` |
| Lines / borders | Smoke | `#3A3835` |
| Accent | Muted gold | `#B8996A` |
| Text | Warm ivory | `#EDE6DA` |

Contrast: ivory on black is **15.1** and gold on black is **7.0**. Both are excellent.

## 3. Shoreline (coastal light)

Feels like a beach or lake resort: bright, airy, and calm.

| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Background | Off-white | `#F7F5F0` |
| Section background | Linen | `#EAE4D8` |
| Soft accent | Sea glass | `#A9BDB5` |
| Muted accent | Driftwood | `#9A8A74` |
| Text / strong accent | Deep navy | `#1F2D3D` |

Contrast: navy on off-white is **12.8** (excellent). Driftwood is **3.1**, so use it for
decoration only.

## 4. Hideaway (earthy and rich)

Feels like a design-forward countryside or jungle hideaway. It's the warmest and most distinctive.

| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Background | Ivory | `#F3EEE4` |
| Accent | Olive | `#5E6146` |
| Highlight | Terracotta | `#B4664A` |
| Deep accent | Walnut | `#5A3E2B` |
| Text | Ink | `#1F1B17` |

Contrast: ink on ivory is **14.8** and olive is **5.5**. Terracotta is **3.7** (large text and buttons only).

## A common approach: mix light and dark

Many luxury sites use a light palette for most pages and switch to a dark section for drama,
like the drone section or a featured project. **Sandstone + Nightcap** pair naturally because
both are warm.

## Type pairing (applies to any palette)

- **Headings:** an elegant serif, such as Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display
- **Body text:** a clean, simple sans-serif, such as Inter or Manrope

Both pairs are free Google Fonts.
