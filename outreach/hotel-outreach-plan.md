# Hotel outreach plan: San Diego

Goal: build a strong hotel portfolio with **4 free shoots max**, then turn the work into paid clients.

## The progression

| Phase | Shoots | What you do | What they get | What you get |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Day shoots** | 2 hotels | Swing by for 2–3 hours | 15 edited photos + 1 short reel, delivered in 7 days | Portfolio work, logo on "Worked with", a testimonial |
| **2. Overnight stays** | 2–3 hotels | Stay 1–2 nights, check-in to check-out | 25 edited photos + 3 reels shot "through the guest's eyes" | The full guest journey for your portfolio, plus a comped stay |
| **3. Paid clients** | Ongoing | Pitch with your portfolio and "Worked with" logos | Paid shoots | Revenue |

**Rules so you're not giving everything away:**
- Only 4 free shoots total. After that, everything is paid.
- Cap what you deliver (above). Extra photos or reels are paid add-ons.
- Get it in writing (a simple email is fine): what you'll deliver, when, and that you can use the work in your portfolio and list their name.
- At every free shoot, ask: "If you're happy with these, would you introduce me to anyone at your sister properties?"

## Phase 1: day shoots (pick 2)

Pick small, independent hotels: photogenic spaces, older-looking photos online, and a GM or owner you can actually reach (no corporate brand approval needed).

| Hotel | Area | Why it's a good first shoot |
|---|---|---|
| **The Pearl Hotel** | Point Loma | Retro mid-century boutique with a pool. Very visual, Instagram-friendly. |

## Phase 2: overnight stays (pick 2–3)

Pick hotels with enough amenities to tell a full guest story: arrival, room, pool, dining, spa, sunset, the area around the hotel.

## Watch out: your day job

You've worked at **La Jolla Shores Hotel**. Before pitching nearby La Jolla hotels (La Valencia, Grande
Colonial, Pantai Inn, La Jolla Cove Hotel, Estancia, The Lodge at Torrey Pines), check your employer's
policy on side work for competitors. Also get their OK before listing La Jolla Shores Hotel or
The Marine Room under "Worked with".

## Before you reach out

These details came from general knowledge and quick searches. Spend a minute checking each hotel's
website and recent Google reviews to confirm it's open, who the GM or marketing contact is, and how
dated its current photos look. Weak current photos = your best pitch.

## Short pitch (Phase 1 and 2)

> Hi [Name], I'm Justin. I've worked in hotel marketing and now shoot hotel photo, video and drone
> content in San Diego. I'm building my portfolio and would love to photograph [Hotel]. In exchange,
> you'd get [15 edited photos and a short reel] to use anywhere: your website, Instagram and ads. No
> cost. Would you be open to a quick visit [this week / on a date that works]?
> You can see my work at justinclosemedia.com.

For overnight stays, swap the offer for: "a 1–2 night stay so I can capture the full guest experience,
check-in to check-out, and you'd receive 25 edited photos and 3 reels."

## Website follow-up

After the first two shoots: add a **"Worked with"** section to the site (hotel names or logos, with
permission) and a short testimonial from each.
