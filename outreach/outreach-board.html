San Diego hotel outreach

Justin Close Media · build the portfolio with 4 free shoots, then go paid

Rules
  • Max 4 free shoots. After that, everything is paid.
  • Put it in writing (email is fine): what you deliver, when, and that you can show their name and the photos.
  • Always ask: "Would you introduce me to your sister properties?"

In exchange, you'd get [15 edited photos and a short reel] to use anywhere: your website, Instagram and ads. No cost.

Would you be open to a quick visit [this week / a date that works]? You can see my work at justinclosemedia.com.
